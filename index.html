I am Federica Padovano, a PhD researcher in Applied Mathematics at LJLL, Sorbonne Université, Paris, under the supervision of Prof. Luis Almeida, Dr. Michael Cerezo and Dr. Chiara Villa.

I work in Mathematical Biology: my research focuses on modelling cancer population dynamics under different scenarios, such as chemotherapy, immunotherapy, and interaction with immune cells. See my publications, talks and teaching, or my CV.
