Welcome to my personal webpage :)

I am Federica Padovano, a PhD in Applied Mathematics at LJLL, Sorbonne Université, Paris, under the supervision of Prof. Luis Almeida, Dr. Michael Cerezo and Dr. Chiara Villa. I work in the field of Mathematical Biology: my research focuses on modelling cancer population dynamics under different scenarios, such as chemotherapy, immunotherapy, and interaction with immune cells.
